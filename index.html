Settings

₹

Take-home pay minus what you save. The starting value is 10,500, which is 15,000 pay minus a 4,500 savings goal. Change it when your pay changes.

Items you buy

These become the quick buttons on the add form. Mark an item shared if you split it with your roommate. Your share is what counts as your spending.

Limits per group

Optional. Set a monthly limit for a group. Its bar turns amber at 80% and red when you pass the limit.

Repeats every month

For things that come every month, like a subscription or a regular grocery top-up. They are added to your log the next time you open the app on or after the day you choose.

Changes save on their own.

Backup

Your entries live only on this phone. Export a backup file every week and keep it in your email or Drive. Importing a backup replaces what is on this phone, and you get a few seconds to undo it.
